Privacy Policy

Effective 8 November 2026

Car Price uses your photos and a few details about the car to estimate its value. We keep as little as we can, and we never sell your data or use it for advertising.

Your account

You sign in with Apple. We don't ask Apple for your name or email. Our server keeps a random account number and a one-way hash of the identifier Apple gives us, so we can recognise you next time. We also record that the account had its free valuation, so it isn't given twice.

Photos and car details

  • Photos are sent to our server and to OpenAI, which identifies the make, model, year and condition. We don't store your photos on our server. OpenAI processes them under its API terms: it does not train its models on this data, and it may keep it for up to 30 days to detect abuse.
  • Car details (make, model, year, trim, mileage, condition) and the city or ZIP/postal code you enter are used to search for similar cars for sale. This search is done by OpenAI's web search. Search results for a car and area are cached for 24 hours so repeat checks are fast; the cache isn't linked to your account.
  • Your valuations (results and photo thumbnails) are saved only on your iPhone.

What our server keeps

  • Your valuation balance and a history of changes to it (free valuation, purchases, valuations used, refunds).
  • For each valuation: the car and area searched, the time and its cost to us, so a re-check within 24 hours is free and so we can answer billing questions.
  • App Store purchase records: transaction numbers, product and date, which Apple sends us to confirm purchases and refunds. We never see your payment details.
  • A device key from Apple's App Attest, which shows requests come from the real app.
  • Your IP address, briefly, to limit how many requests one connection can make per day (kept for about a day), and in short-lived server logs used to fix problems.

Who processes data for us

  • Cloudflare runs our server and database.
  • OpenAI identifies the car from your photos and searches for similar cars for sale. The app asks your permission before it sends anything to OpenAI, and you can turn this off in Account.
  • Apple handles sign-in and purchases.

We don't use advertising or analytics SDKs, and we don't track you across apps or websites.

Deleting your data

Open the Account tab, then Delete account. We delete your account, balance, balance history, valuation records and device keys straight away. Purchase records stay, no longer linked to you, because we must keep sales records; the hashed Apple identifier stays only to show that the free valuation was already given. Deleting the app removes everything saved on your iPhone.

Children

Car Price isn't directed at children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app. Questions or requests: topsol.org@gmail.com.
