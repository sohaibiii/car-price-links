Support

Car Price tells you what a used car is worth. Take a few photos, confirm the car, and we compare it with similar cars for sale near you.

How valuations work

  • Sign in with Apple to start. Your first valuation is free.
  • A valuation is used only when we find a price. If we can't find enough similar cars for sale, nothing is used.
  • Checking the same car again within 24 hours is free.
  • Valuations you buy don't expire. The Dealer plan adds 50 valuations each month until you cancel.

Purchases

Purchases are made through the App Store and tied to the Apple Account you signed in with. On a new iPhone, sign in with the same Apple Account and tap Restore purchases on the purchase screen.

To cancel Dealer, open Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your iPhone. For a refund, use reportaproblem.apple.com.

Delete your account

In the app, tap the account icon on the home screen, then Delete account. This removes your account, your remaining valuations and your valuation records from our server. Valuations saved on your iPhone stay until you delete the app.

Contact

Email topsol.org@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model and, if it's about a valuation, the car's make, model and year. We usually reply within two working days.
